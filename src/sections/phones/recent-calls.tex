\section{Recent Call In Dialing}
You can view the placed calls list when the phone is in the pre-dialing
screen.

\subsubsection{To view placed calls list when the phone is in the pre-dialing screen:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Pick up the handset, press the speakerphone or press the line key.

		The LCD screen displays the placed calls list.

		\begin{figure}[h]
			\centering
			\includegraphics{images/yealink/screen-004.png}
		\end{figure}
\end{enumerate}
